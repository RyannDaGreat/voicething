RESEARCH RESULTS - KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

BUBBLE EFFECTS
──────────────
How to create convincing water bubbles:
- Use QRadialGradient with 4 color stops
- White center → Cyan mid → Dark Blue edge → Shadow rim
- Alpha values: 255 → 220 → 180 → 100 (decreasing transparency)
- Highlight offset: 0.3 × radius from center (creates 3D effect)
- Result: Realistic sphere appearance with light reflection


WET SURFACE TECHNIQUE
─────────────────────
How to make surfaces look wet and glossy:
- Layer 1: Color gradient (light to dark)
- Layer 2: White frosted overlay at 35% opacity (MOST IMPORTANT)
- Layer 3: Bright specular highlight (tight gradient at 30% from top-left)
- Layer 4: Soft shadow below using Multiply blend mode
- Result: Convincing "water on glass" appearance


AQUATIC COLOR PALETTE
─────────────────────
Colors that make UI feel aquatic:
- Primary: Cyan #00C8FF (RGB 0, 200, 255)
- Secondary: Turquoise #40E0D0 (RGB 64, 224, 208)
- Light: Light Aqua #AFEEEE (RGB 175, 238, 238)
- Dark: Dark Navy #003366 (RGB 0, 51, 102)
- Highlight: White #FFFFFF or Light Cyan #E6FFFF
- Result: Blue-green palette immediately reads as "aquatic"


ANIMATION SPECIFICATIONS
────────────────────────
How to animate bubbles naturally:
- Duration: 3-8 seconds per bubble
- Float: 150px upward over duration
- Drift: ±30px horizontal (sine wave = organic motion)
- Opacity: Fade in (first 20%) → visible → fade out (last 20%)
- Timer: 40ms interval = 25fps (smooth, performant)
- Max: 20 bubbles (more is distracting)


WINDOWS AERO REFERENCE
──────────────────────
Why Aero looked wet/aquatic:
- Glass morphism (semi-transparent layering)
- Specular highlights (bright reflections)
- Soft shadows (depth perception)
- Color gradients (light to dark transitions)
- Cool blue-cyan palette (evokes water)
- Smooth transitions (no hard edges)

EXACT ANSWERS TO YOUR QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q1: How to paint convincing bubble/water droplet effects in PyQt6?
A:  Use QRadialGradient with 4 color stops:
    - Center: White RGB(255,255,255) alpha 255
    - 40%: Cyan RGB(100,200,255) alpha 220
    - 70%: Dark Blue RGB(50,150,200) alpha 180
    - 100%: Shadow RGB(30,80,150) alpha 100
    Highlight offset at 0.3×radius creates 3D sphere effect.
    See: aquatic_effects_example.py draw_bubble()


Q2: How to make surfaces look wet and reflective?
A:  Stack these 4 layers:
    1. Color gradient (light to dark)
    2. White frosted overlay (35% opacity) - most important
    3. Specular highlight (bright, tight, positioned 30% from top-left)
    4. Soft shadow below (Multiply blend mode)
    Result: Convincing "water on glass" appearance.
    See: AQUATIC_FRUTIGER_AERO_GUIDE.md Section 2


Q3: What makes Frutiger Aero look aquatic?
A:  Three things:
    1. Blue-green color palette (cyan/turquoise dominant)
    2. Layered transparency (depth + glass effect)
    3. Floating bubbles (organic water-like motion)
    Together: User immediately perceives "aquatic" aesthetic.
    See: AQUATIC_COLORS_VISUAL_REFERENCE.txt


Q4: What did authentic Windows Vista/7 Aero look like?
A:  Glass Morphism:
    - Semi-opaque white overlay
    - Light gradient (top to bottom)
    - Colored base layer (cyan/blue)
    - Soft shadows for depth
    - Cool blue palette
    Colors: #E6F2FF (light), #4A7BA7 (medium), #FFFFFF (highlight)
    See: AQUATIC_FRUTIGER_AERO_GUIDE.md Section 4


Q5: How to add floating bubbles without being distracting?
A:  Strategy:
    - Place in corners/edges, not center
    - Use 5-20 bubbles total
    - Vary sizes (5px, 15px, 30px)
    - Opacity: 60-80% (visible but transparent)
    - Animation: 3-8 seconds duration
    - Float upward + gentle side drift
    See: INTEGRATION_STEPS.md
