KEY FINDINGS SUMMARY
================================================================================

WINAMP CLASSIC:
  Waveform Color: #00FF00 (pure bright green)
  Background: #1a1a1a (deep dark)
  Visualization: Spectrum bars + oscilloscope waveform
  Update Rate: 30-60 FPS
  Font: Verdana or MS Sans Serif

WINDOWS 95:
  ButtonFace: #C0C0C0 (silver-gray)
  Highlight: #FFFFFF (top/left edge)
  Shadow: #808080 (bottom/right edge)
  DkShadow: #000000 (deep shadow)
  Beveling: border-style outset/inset
  
Y2K AESTHETIC:
  Primary: Metallic gradients (#FFFFFF → #C0C0C0 → #808080)
  Gold Accents: #FFD700
  Bright Silver: #E8E8E8
  Glow Effects: Subtle transparency

VAPORWAVE:
  Hot Pink: #FF71CE
  Cyan: #01CDFE
  Purple: #B967FF
  Mint: #05FFA1
  Pale Yellow: #FFFB96
  Best Use: Spectrum visualization gradients

================================================================================
TYPOGRAPHY
================================================================================

Primary Font: Verdana (body, buttons, labels)
  Size: 10-11pt normal, 9pt small labels
  Weight: Bold for buttons and headers

Monospace Font: Consolas (technical display)
  Size: 9-10pt
  Use: Time codes, file info, technical readouts

Alternative/Accent Font: Futura or Montserrat (Y2K feel)
  Size: 12pt+ for headers only
  Use: Titles, main headings

Typography Rules:
  - Keep fonts consistent (2-3 families max)
  - Dark text on light, light text on dark
  - Gold text for titles on dark backgrounds
  - Avoid thin weights at small sizes
  - Bold weights recommended for buttons
