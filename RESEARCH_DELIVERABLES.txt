KEY INFORMATION SUMMARY
================================================================================

RECOMMENDATION: Aero Glass (Vista/Win7) Style
Why: Aligns with Frutiger Aero, uses blue palette, modern but retro

COLORS:
  Title bar top:    rgba(232, 244, 248, 100)
  Title bar mid:    rgba(176, 208, 224, 140)
  Title bar bottom: rgba(0, 120, 200, 200)
  Button hover:     rgba(176, 208, 224, 160)
  Button close:     rgba(255, 100, 100, 180)

DIMENSIONS:
  Title bar height: 28-30 pixels
  Button size: 18x16 pixels

BUTTON GLYPHS:
  Minimize: ─ (U+2500)
  Maximize: □ (U+25A1)
  Close: ✕ (U+2715)

IMPLEMENTATION:
  1. Copy TITLEBAR_QUICK_START.py classes
  2. Remove native frame: setWindowFlags(Qt.WindowType.FramelessWindowHint)
  3. Use AeroGlassTitleBar in your window
  4. Connect buttons to window actions
  5. Test thoroughly

  Basic implementation: 1-2 days
  Full featured: 3-5 days
  Production ready: 1 week
